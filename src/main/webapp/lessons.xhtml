Lesson scheduling

How the clash check works. A booking is refused when the student, the instructor or the vehicle is already committed to an overlapping slot on the same date. Cancelled lessons release their resources and never block a new booking. Back-to-back lessons are allowed: 09:00-10:00 and 10:00-11:00 do not overlap.

All three resources must be free for the whole slot.

You need at least one student, one instructor and one vehicle before a lesson can be booked. Use the Students and the Instructors and vehicles pages first.

Format yyyy-MM-dd
Format HH:mm, for example 09:00
Must be later than the start time

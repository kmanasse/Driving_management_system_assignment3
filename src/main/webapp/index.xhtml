What this system does

Phase 1 deliverable

Driving schools schedule practical lessons by hand, usually in a paper diary. Because a lesson needs a student, an instructor and a vehicle all free at the same time, the same instructor or the same car is regularly promised to two students at once. The clash is only discovered when both turn up.

This system replaces the diary. Every booking is checked against every other booking before it is accepted, so a double booking is refused at the moment it is made rather than discovered at the kerb.

Getting started

Follow these three steps in order the first time you run the application

  1. Open Instructors and vehicles and register at least one instructor and one vehicle. A lesson cannot be booked without them.
  2. Open Students and enrol a student. Try a date of birth less than 18 years ago to see the custom validator reject it.
  3. Open Lesson schedule and book a lesson. Then book a second one that overlaps it to see the double booking check refuse it.

Entities with full CRUD

Student and Lesson, as required by the assignment

The remaining entities in the class diagram - Instructor, Vehicle, Payment and Mock Exam - are mapped by Hibernate and created in the database, but only Student and Lesson have complete create, read, update and delete screens.
